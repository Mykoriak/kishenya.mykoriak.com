Privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026

The short version. Your entries, accounts, categories and budgets are stored on your iPhone. You can use Kishenya without an account and offline. Data leaves your phone only for the optional features below: an account and AI parsing, cloud sync, and bug reports you choose to send. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and your data is never sold.

Who is responsible

Kishenya is made by Ivan Mykoriak, an independent developer in Ukraine (“I”, “me”). I am the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: kishenyasupport@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your iPhone

  • Your entries (amounts, dates, notes), accounts and balances, categories, budgets, currencies and exchange rates, and your settings.
  • Backup files you export. They go only where you save them, for example iCloud Drive.
  • Your voice. Speech is turned into text by iOS on your iPhone, and Kishenya never uploads audio.

If you use iCloud Backup, iOS may include the app’s data in your device backup. Apple handles that under its own privacy policy.

When data leaves your iPhone

An account (optional)

You only need an account for AI parsing (and cloud sync, where it’s offered). You can sign in with email and password, Google, or Apple. I store your email address, your name if Google or Apple passes it, an account ID, and which sign-in method you used. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash by the sign-in service. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your real email address.

AI parsing

Kishenya first tries to understand a phrase on your iPhone. Only when it can’t, only if you are signed in, and only after you allow it, the app sends this to my server:

  • the phrase you typed or dictated (up to 500 characters),
  • today’s date, your base currency, the currency codes you use and the app’s language,
  • the names of your categories and accounts, so the answer can use them.

The first time a phrase needs AI, the app asks you first and shows what would be sent, who gets it and what is kept. Nothing is sent until you tap Allow. If you tap Not now, the app keeps working with what your iPhone understood. You can turn AI parsing on or off at any time in Settings → AI parsing.

My server passes this to OpenAI to get the amount, category, account, note and date back, and returns the result to the app. I don’t store the phrase or the result. I store how many AI requests your account made each day, to enforce the limit of 100 a day. OpenAI processes the request as my service provider. It says that data sent through its API isn’t used to train its models and may be kept for up to 30 days to detect abuse.

Cloud sync (only if it’s enabled for your account)

When sync is on, your accounts, categories, entries (including notes) and budgets are copied to my server so they appear on your other devices. Each row is tied to your account, and database rules let only your account read it.

Bug reports (only when you send one)

Settings → Report a bug sends:

  • your message, and your email address if you add one,
  • a screenshot, if you attach one,
  • diagnostics, which you can read in the form before sending: app version and build, iOS version, device model, app language, base currency, database version, a random ID for this installation, your account ID if you are signed in, and the app’s recent warnings and errors.

A bug report never contains your entries, notes, amounts, balances, or account or category names. The report is stored on my server, and a copy is sent to my private Telegram chat through a bot so I see it quickly. To stop abuse, the server stores a keyed hash of your network address with the report, never the address itself.

Exchange rates and app updates

The app downloads official exchange rates from the National Bank of Ukraine (bank.gov.ua). When it starts, it also asks Expo’s update service whether a newer version of the app’s code is available. Both requests carry only technical details (such as the app and iOS version and, for updates, a random installation ID) and your network address, like any request on the internet. No personal data from the app is sent.

What I don’t do

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no data brokers.
  • I don’t sell or rent your data, and I don’t share it for advertising.
  • I don’t use your data to train AI models.

Why I may use your data (legal basis)

Account, AI parsing and sync: to provide the features you ask for (performance of a contract). Bug reports: to fix problems you report (your request and my legitimate interest in a working app). Request limits and the hashed network address: my legitimate interest in preventing abuse.

How long data is kept

  • Account, synced data and AI request counts: until you delete your account.
  • Bug reports and screenshots: until the problem is handled, and no longer than 12 months.
  • Phrases sent for AI parsing: not stored by me. OpenAI: see above.

Deleting your data

On your iPhone: deleting the app deletes all its data. Export a backup first if you want to keep it.

Your account: in Settings, tap Delete account in the account card at the top. This deletes your account and everything stored with it on my server right away: synced data, AI request counts, and the account ID on your bug reports (the reports themselves stay, without it). If you signed in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm, and the app revokes Kishenya’s access to your Apple ID. The data on your iPhone stays; delete the app to remove it. Deleting needs an internet connection.

You can also email kishenyasupport@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and I’ll delete your account within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or Ukraine’s law on personal data protection), you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal data, object to its use, or restrict it. Email me and I’ll answer within 30 days. Settings → Backup & restore gives you a full copy of what’s on your iPhone at any time. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Kishenya isn’t directed at children under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect their personal data. If you think a child has given me personal data, email me and I’ll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, I’ll publish the new version here with a new date. For significant changes, the app will tell you too.
